Privacy Policy

Arch Studio · Last updated: 2026

Summary

Arch Studio is built around a single promise: your play stays on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no notifications, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.

1. Information We Collect

Arch Studio does not collect any personal information from you. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or account credentials
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprinting data
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral telemetry
  • Contacts, calendars, or any data outside the app

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your iPhone only. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it:

  • Game progress — stars, best times, moves, two-player match records
  • Bridge Gallery entries — timestamps, snapshots, and route metadata for every completed crossing
  • Custom deck tiles — photos you choose for your personal deck
  • Free-build levels — arches, color mixes, and anchor cells you design
  • App settings — haptics, theme, and reset preferences

All of this is stored using standard iOS app sandbox storage and is removed when you delete the app or tap the one-tap Reset inside the app.

3. Photos and Permissions

Arch Studio requests photo library access only when you choose to build a custom deck or save a finished snapshot to your photo library. Specifically:

  • Photo Library Read — used solely to import a photo you pick and convert it into a personal bridge tile. The source photo is processed on-device.
  • Photo Library Write — used solely to save a completed bridge snapshot to your library at your request.
  • Camera — if you opt to capture a quick reference for a custom tile, the captured image stays on-device inside the app.

Photos are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted. You may deny any of these permissions and the rest of the app continues to work normally.

4. Network and Tracking

Arch Studio makes no network calls for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, account syncing, or any other purpose. The app functions fully offline. No third-party SDKs that perform tracking are bundled into the build.

5. Children

Arch Studio does not target children and does not knowingly collect any information from children. Because no information is collected from anyone, this policy applies uniformly across all users.

6. Your Choices and Controls

You are always able to:

  • Delete all in-app progress with the one-tap Reset in Settings
  • Remove individual custom tiles from your deck at any time
  • Revoke photo or camera permissions from iOS Settings
  • Delete the app to remove all on-device data immediately

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Because the app does not collect data, any future change is unlikely to weaken your privacy.
