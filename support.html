Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?
Tap any card to swap it with an adjacent card. Build adjacent same-color chains to raise the arch and create a closed route for the toy car to cross.
Where is my progress stored?
All progress lives locally on your iPhone. Arch Studio does not sync progress to any server.
How do I reset everything?
Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Reset. All stars, times, gallery entries, custom tiles, and free-build levels will be cleared.
Can I build a deck from my own photos?
Yes. Open the Deck Builder, grant photo permission when prompted, and pick any photo from your library. It becomes a personal tile inside your private deck.
Does the app need an internet connection?
No. Arch Studio is fully offline. There are no accounts, no ads, and no background network calls.
How does the two-player mode work?
Two players take turns on the same board, hot-seated. The dedicated results pane shows stars and time for each player.
Can I delete a custom tile?
Yes. Open the Deck Builder, find the tile you want to remove, and swipe it away. It is removed from your device immediately.
What devices are supported?
Arch Studio runs on iPhone with iOS 16.1 or later, in portrait orientation.

Reporting a Bug

When emailing us about a problem, please include:

1. Your iPhone model and iOS version.
2. The screen or level where the issue occurred.
3. A short description of what happened and what you expected to happen.

Send the details to dhfu94086@gmail.com and we will investigate.
