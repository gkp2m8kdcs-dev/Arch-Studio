About Arch Studio

Arch Studio is a premium puzzle atelier for iPhone — a calm table of toy cards and toy cars, twenty hand-designed bridges deep. Players swap adjacent cards to raise and dismantle toy bridge arches; each card's value shapes how high the arch stands, and adjacent cards bundle together so a tiny car can roll the full route.

Same-color chains form Misty-grade transitions, and every closed route sends a smooth car animation across the deck. Four landscape packs — Coastal River, Mountain Gorge, City Skyline, and Twilight Canyon — unlock progressively and reward thoughtful planning over speed.

Privacy Promise

Arch Studio ships with no accounts, no in-app purchases, no advertising, no notifications, and no tracking. Every metric lives on-device only. A one-tap Reset wipes all progress for the next player. Custom deck photos stay inside a private deck and are never uploaded to a server.
